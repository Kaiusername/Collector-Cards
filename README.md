# Collector Cards
Collect hand-painted cards inspired by the world and community of THE LONG DARK!

# Features:
- 5 (+25 more coming) Cards to Collect
- Card Packs to Open
- 'Variants' such as Foil and Golds
- Hand Painted, no AI
- Vanilla Friendly
- Localization Friendly

Download the .modcomponent and drop it into your 'mods' folder.
